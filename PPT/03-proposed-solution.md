# Slide 3 — Proposed Solution

## What is KalaSetu?
An **AI-powered, cross-platform mobile app** that acts as a **"virtual business manager"** for
artisans — it digitizes their products, creates professional listings, and connects them to
year-round digital markets, with **no technical skill required**.

## The 30-second magic (how it removes the 3 walls)
1. **Snap** a photo of the product.
2. **Speak** a description in your own regional language (or type).
3. The AI instantly produces a **professional, priced, ready-to-sell listing** in **English + Hindi**.
4. **Publish** — the product goes live to **B2B buyers and government e-marketplaces (ONDC / GeM)**.

## What makes it unique (Innovation)
- **Voice-first + bilingual + icon-driven** — built specifically for **low-literacy** users.
- **One tap turns a phone photo into a studio-quality catalog listing** with a fair price.
- **Offline-first** — works on low-end phones and poor networks (drafts sync later).
- **Read-aloud (text-to-speech)** on every screen so users can *hear* the app.

**Speaker note:**
"Instead of teaching an artisan e-commerce, we let the AI do it. They just point, speak, and
sell — and the app handles the photo, the description, the price, and the marketplace."
