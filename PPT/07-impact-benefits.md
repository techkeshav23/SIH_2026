# Slide 7 — Impact & Benefits

## Impact on the target audience (marginalized artisans)
- **Year-round income:** a continuous digital sales channel — **no longer dependent** on a few
  physical fairs each year.
- **Lower barrier to digital commerce:** AI automation removes the need for photography,
  writing, pricing or English skills.
- **Higher & steadier earnings:** professional listings + fair pricing + **direct B2B / govt buyers**
  → improved financial independence.

## Benefits
- **Social:** digital & financial inclusion of marginalized communities; **improved digital literacy**.
- **Economic:** increased average annual income for artisans; direct market access without middlemen.
- **Environmental:** promotes **sustainable, handmade** crafts over mass production.

## Scale & alignment
- Serves **MoSJE's large beneficiary base** (micro-entrepreneurs, artisans, weavers across India).
- Aligns with **Digital India, Atmanirbhar Bharat, ONDC and Bhashini** national missions.

**Speaker note:**
"The real impact is turning a few days of fair income into a stable, year-round livelihood —
while pulling marginalized artisans into the digital economy in their own language."
