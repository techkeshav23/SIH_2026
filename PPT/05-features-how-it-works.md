# Slide 5 — Key Features / How It Works

## The 3 core AI features (as required by the problem statement)
1. **AI Image Studio** — a built-in camera that **removes cluttered backgrounds, corrects lighting,
   and crops** photos to clean, professional e-commerce standards (before → after).
2. **Multilingual Voice Auto-Cataloger** — the artisan **speaks in a regional language**; the AI
   transcribes, translates, and writes a **SEO-friendly listing in English + Hindi** automatically.
3. **Dynamic Pricing Assistant** — an ML model reads the product and **real market comparables**
   to suggest an **optimal, competitive price range** with clear, honest reasoning.

## Full app also includes (built and working in demo)
- **B2B Marketplace** (ONDC/GeM-styled) with buyer discovery, **search & category filters**, and **bulk inquiries**.
- **Buyer side:** product pages, **cart & multi-item checkout**, **order → approval → payment** flow, **ratings & reviews**.
- **Artisan side:** product management, **incoming orders (accept/reject)**, **order tracking timeline**, **earnings dashboard**, shop profile.
- **Accessibility:** **read-aloud (TTS)** on every screen, **large-text mode**, Hindi/English toggle.
- **Offline mode:** create product drafts without internet; they **sync automatically** later.
- **Notifications centre** for orders, payments and inquiries.

**Speaker note:**
"The three AI features solve photo, description and price. Around them we built a complete
marketplace — buyers, orders, payments, reviews — all usable offline and in Hindi with voice."
