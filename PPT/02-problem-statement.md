# Slide 2 — Problem Statement (The Gap We Solve)

## Background
- The government supports marginalized artisans, weavers and micro-entrepreneurs through
  financial aid and physical exhibitions (Shilp Samagam, Surajkund Mela, Dilli Haat).
- These fairs give only a **temporary** boost in sales — for a few days a year.

## The core problem
- **No year-round market:** between fairs, sales fall to almost nothing.
- Artisans **cannot** join online marketplaces on their own because of three walls:
  1. **Low digital literacy** — they can't build or manage online listings.
  2. **Language barrier** — most content and platforms are in English.
  3. **No cataloging skills** — they can't take professional photos, write descriptions, or price competitively.

## Result
- Heavy **dependency on periodic physical fairs**, unstable and low annual income,
  and no path into the digital economy.

**Speaker note:**
"Artisans make beautiful products, but once a mela ends, their income stops. They have no
easy way to sell online — the technology, the language, and the skills are all barriers."
